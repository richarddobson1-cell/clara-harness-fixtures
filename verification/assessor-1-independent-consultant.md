## Assessor finding

The single telemetry hit is a percent sign in reply 5. The assessor noted that it appears as an algebraic placeholder — "M%", naming a hypothetical threshold inside a reasoning move — rather than as a measurement or telemetry figure reported to a learner, and flagged the case as one where the mechanical verdict and the screen's intent diverge: the pattern fired correctly, but on a use the telemetry screen was not built to catch. The finding is recorded here, and discussed in the paper (§4.11), as a screen over-match bearing on the telemetry screen's precision, not corrected away.
